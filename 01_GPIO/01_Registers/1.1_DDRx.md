# DDRx — Data Direction Register

The **DDRx (Data Direction Register)** is used to configure the direction of GPIO pins in an AVR microcontroller.

Each bit of the DDRx register controls one GPIO pin.

- `0` → Configure pin as **Input**
- `1` → Configure pin as **Output**

## Register Structure

### DDRx

| Bit | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-----|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pin | Px7 | Px6 | Px5 | Px4 | Px3 | Px2 | Px1 | Px0 |
